From 1981 to 1983, the Corleonesi faction led by Salvatore Riina and Bernardo Provenzano waged a brutal internal war against rival Cosa Nostra families in Sicily. Hundreds died—Mafia members, civilians, and anyone in between. The Corleonesi won. Their victory reshaped Cosa Nostra into a more violent, centralized, and reckless organization. The peace that followed wasn't peace. It was control.

In this video: how the war started, the tactics that won it, and how one small-town faction came to dominate Sicily's underworld.
